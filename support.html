Where are my files?

Open the Files app → On My iPhone → MP3 Diva. Added media is in Imported, everything you export is in Library. In the app, find them under the Files tab.

Which formats are supported?

Input: most videos and audio files iOS can play (MP4, MOV, M4V, MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, FLAC, CAF…). Output: MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, AIFF, CAF and M4R ringtones.

How do I use a ringtone I made?

Share the M4R file to GarageBand and export it as a ringtone, or sync it to your iPhone with Finder on a Mac.

Subscriptions

MP3 Diva Pro unlocks every tool and unlimited exports. To cancel, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. To restore a purchase on a new device, tap Restore Purchases in the app's Settings tab.
