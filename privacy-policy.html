mp3Diva Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

mp3Diva is built to work entirely on your iPhone. This policy explains what that means for your data.

We don't collect your data

mp3Diva has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information, and we do not share anything with third parties.

Your files stay on your device

Videos and audio you add, and everything you export, are processed and saved only on your iPhone (in the app's folder, which you can also see in the Files app). Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes these files.

Photos and Files

When you add media, you choose the exact items in Apple's system pickers. mp3Diva only receives the files you pick and never gets access to the rest of your library.

Links you paste

If you add media from a link, your iPhone downloads the file directly from that website. We don't see or log the link. That website may receive standard connection data (such as your IP address) under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Children

mp3Diva does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions? Email us at mpdivasupport@gmail.com.
