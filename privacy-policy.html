mp3Diva Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

mp3Diva is built to work on your iPhone. Your media never leaves your device. The only information that leaves it is what's needed to manage your subscription, as explained below.

What we collect

mp3Diva has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never collect your name, email, contacts, location or the media you work with, and we never sell any information.

Your files stay on your device

Videos and audio you add, and everything you export, are processed and saved only on your iPhone (in the app's folder, which you can also see in the Files app). Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes these files.

Photos and Files

When you add media, you choose the exact items in Apple's system pickers. mp3Diva only receives the files you pick and never gets access to the rest of your library.

Purchases

Apple handles payment through the App Store, and we never see your payment details. To check whether your subscription is active, mp3Diva uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous ID created by the app (not linked to your name or Apple ID)
  • your purchase and subscription history (product, price, dates, renewals, trial status)
  • basic technical data: device model, iOS version, app version, country and language, and your IP address

This information is used only to unlock Pro, restore purchases and keep subscriptions working. It is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps and websites. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy. Apple's privacy policy applies to the payment itself.

Your choices

You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. To ask us to delete the subscription data linked to your anonymous ID, email us. Include the date of your purchase so we can find the record.

Children

mp3Diva is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date above.

Contact
